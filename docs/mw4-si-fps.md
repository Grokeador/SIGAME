## What the preset does
Edits MW4's own settings file (s.1.0.cod26.txt) for max FPS. Reads your PC: render threads = your physical cores minus one, video memory budget from your VRAM, FPS cap 3 under your refresh rate, low-latency mode for your GPU (Reflex + boost, Anti-Lag 2 or XeLL). Sets hidden keys the menu does not have (corpses, blood, reflection relighting, surface subdivision, shader pre-load at maximum, power-saving drops off, cloud sync off so nothing overwrites it, no reset after a driver update) and the heavy graphics to their lowest. Only keys in your file, only values the file allows. Close the game first. Backup first, UNDO puts it back.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI FPS (Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 (PC))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
