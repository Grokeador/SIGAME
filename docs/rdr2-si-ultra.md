## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI ULTRA (Red Dead Redemption 2 (Parley AI NPCs + Vulkan Frame Generation mods))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
