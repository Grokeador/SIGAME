## What the preset does
Writes the REFramework keys that live outside the game menu, picked for your screen: vignette off, field of view 90 on 16:9, or on 21:9 and wider the ultrawide fix with the HUD kept in 16:9. Needs REFramework (free, MIT) in the game folder. Backup first, UNDO puts it back.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI ULTRA (Resident Evil Requiem (REFramework, RE Engine))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
