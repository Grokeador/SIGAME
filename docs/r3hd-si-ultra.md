## What the preset does
Writes two engine keys the port's Settings app does not show, at the highest value its runtime accepts: 16x anisotropic filtering (default 4x) and FXAA Extreme, in bin\r3hd.toml. Also sets Rendering resolution 2160p in settings.json. Close the game first. Backup first, UNDO puts it back.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI ULTRA (Rayman 3 HD (Rayman 3 HD Recompiled PC port))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
