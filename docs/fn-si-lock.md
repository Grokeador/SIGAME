- Result: Keeps YOUR graphics settings when Fortnite resets them.

## What the preset does
Keeps YOUR graphics settings when Fortnite resets them. Clears the read-only flag on GameUserSettings.ini (Epic's own fix), saves a copy of your settings and adds a hidden check every 15 minutes: after a Fortnite update changes your file, your copy goes back; when you change settings in the menu, the copy follows you. UNDO removes the check and the copy.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI LOCK (Fortnite (Epic Games, PC))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
