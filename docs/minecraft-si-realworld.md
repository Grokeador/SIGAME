## What the preset does
Writes the RealWorldMC keys its map menu cannot show: mapRenderDistance 24000 (the hidden camera far plane, default 8000, cuts every tile past 8000 blocks even with Far = 24000), falloff 1200 (far tiles twice as sharp, default 600) and viewportHeight = your screen height (detail was computed for 1080 lines), plus Far 24000 and Max tiles 2400. Your map keys are kept. Close Minecraft first. Backup first, UNDO puts it back.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI REAL WORLD (Minecraft: RealWorldMC (real-world 3D map mod by Teddy563, Fabric))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
