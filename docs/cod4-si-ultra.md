## What the preset does
Configures CoD4's own profile configs (config.cfg + config_mp.cfg) with keys the options menu does not have, inside the ranges the game itself registers: FPS cap = your monitor's Hz (default 85), FOV 80 (the game's maximum, default 65), full-detail models and soldiers far away (LOD bias by your video memory), texture size forced to full on colour/normal/specular/water maps, 16x anisotropic, shadow maps on all 4 primary lights, anti-aliasing by your video memory. Close the game first. Backup first, UNDO puts it back.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI ULTRA (Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare (2007, Steam))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.
