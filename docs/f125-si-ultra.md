## What the preset does
Edits F1 25's own hardware_settings_config.xml, only the values the F1-25-RE-Optimization author lists as not clamped by the graphics preset: SSAO algorithm, screen space reflections, clouds, grass distance, car reflection updates. Only ever cheaper, no DLL. Changed a graphics option in the menu? APPLY, No, Yes. Names from that MIT repo. UNDO restores your file.

## How do I apply it?
1. Download SIGAME: https://github.com/Grokeador/SIGAME (Code, Download ZIP, extract).
2. Double-click `SI-GAME-AUTO.bat`.
3. SI PRESETS, Check for new presets, Yes.
4. Close the game, pick **SI ULTRA (F1 25 (PC))**, APPLY, Yes. APPLY again and Yes = Undo.

## FAQ
Q: Is it free?
A: Yes. SIGAME and every SI preset are free, no account.

Q: How do I go back to my old settings?
A: The preset makes a backup first; APPLY again and Yes restores it.

Q: Where do the values come from?
A: From the game's or the port's own files and source: https://github.com/xanpavle/F1-25-RE-Optimization.
